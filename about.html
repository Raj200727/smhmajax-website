Est. 2011 · Ajax, Ontario About Our Mandir

A Sacred Space
For Our Community

Sankat Mochan Hanuman Mandir & Cultural Centre was founded in 2011 by a group of devoted families with roots in the Caribbean and South Asian Hindu tradition. What began as a gathering of faith has grown into a vibrant spiritual home for the entire Durham Region.

Our Leadership & Spiritual Guidance

Pt. Rabindranath Tiwari is the founder and spiritual leader of the Sankat Mochan Hanuman Mandir & Cultural Centre in Ajax. After completing his study of the Vedas and Puranas in Rishikesh, India, in 2001, he dedicated his life to community service and spiritual care. In addition to performing traditional Pujas, weddings, and rites of passage, he serves as a hospital chaplain, Vedanta teacher, and compassionate advisor to people of all faiths.
